The same reading model as a conversation: Up/Down walks blocks, Rewind/FF page. Internal links are focusable on their own; OK on devices.md opens that note, a link to a visualization opens the Viewer.

Fire TV archie/devices/fire-tv.md

Fire TV Stick 4K

The living-room stick (AFTKM, Fire OS 8) runs the launcher and Archie TV. Its address comes from DHCP, so scripts find it by MAC; see devices.md for the full device list.

How Archie reaches it

  • Agents open pages through the TV app's control socket: tv_cmd.py viz <path>.
  • adb stays as the fallback for launching other apps and screenshots; see connect-tv.
  • The mic button belongs to Archie once tv_setup.sh has turned off Alexa's voice UI; Remote mic probe has the numbers.

Display

1920 × 1080 at 320 dpi, so 960 × 540 dp. Keep everything inside 48 dp left and right, 27 dp top and bottom.
